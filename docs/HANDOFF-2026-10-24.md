## Safe resume

Read `CLAUDE.md`, then this handoff and the two feature documents. Start with:

```powershell
pwsh -NoProfile -File tools\winsnipper.ps1 status
```

`--capture-probe` is an operational test: it can clear a deliberate-quit marker
and start the app. It is not a passive status query. Supervised builds/restarts
require the owner's process-stop approval. Do not publish into `dist` manually,
forge Windows default-choice hashes, close other apps, or infer terminal closure
permission from "wrap up". No apps or terminals were closed during this handoff.
