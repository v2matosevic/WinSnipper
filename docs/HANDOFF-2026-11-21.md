## What to look at after installing

All in `%APPDATA%\WinSnipper`:

- `session.log` startup block should now have four lines: `startup`,
  `residency min=`, `capture-surface locked=`, `memory-trim private=A->B`.
- `memory-trim` lines after editor sessions. Private memory should stay near
  its post-start value instead of ratcheting past 700 MB.
- `ui-stall` lines name the stage of any freeze of 1.5 s or more. This is the
  only evidence path for the three "not responding" closures; their cause is
  still unknown.
- `runtime-removed restarting=true` after the next .NET update (roughly
  monthly), followed by a new `startup`.
- `recorder.log` `stats` line after each recording: `late-slots` above zero
  means the capture loop could not keep up (visible stutter); `unique` is the
  number of frames with new content.
- Snips 15 to 60 minutes after the previous use: the screen copy
  (`capture-ready` minus `capture-started`) should no longer take 0.7 to 3.9 s.

## Not verified

- Anything in real use: the fixes are judged by the log lines above.
- The self-restart after a real runtime update. The detector and the mutex
  hand-over are tested separately; a real update has not happened since.
  Whether a process started by the elevated keep-alive task may leave a child
  running when it exits is assumed from Task Scheduler's normal behaviour; if
  it does not, the two-minute watchdog relaunches the app instead.
- Pasting into specific applications. The clipboard holds a standard CF_DIB,
  from which Windows derives CF_BITMAP and CF_DIBV5; verified by round trip
  and a WPF reader, not in Slack, Chrome, Office and so on.
- The drop-down lists in Settings open a popup; rendered closed only.
- Recording smoothness as the owner sees it, and full-screen games.
